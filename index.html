Everything in the app

Every Moodcorn feature, on one page.

🎬Find by mood

  • 13 moods, pick up to 2

    Choose two and Mısır puts the titles that fit both at the top.

  • Genre, length and family

    18 genres or "Anything goes". Say "I have 1.5 hours" or turn on "Watching with family".

  • Match score

    Every card shows "Mood match: 4.6/5", a Moodcorn score 🍿 out of 10 and a "Why this pick?" line.

  • Details

    Spoiler-free summary, where to watch, trailer, a snack idea, "Start now and you're done by 11:10 PM" and 3 similar titles.

  • Search, cast and directors

    Search any title, or tap an actor or director to see their other work.

  • Roll the dice, swipe the cards

    Can't decide? Roll the dice or swipe: right for My list, left to skip.

💬Talk to Mısır

  • Write to Mısır

    Type what you want, like "a Korean series that won't make me cry". Mısır gets the mood, genre, length, who you're watching with, the country and what to avoid. Offline, in 6 languages.

  • Show Mısır

    Snap your room, the rain or your cat. Mısır reads the scene and picks "this photo's movie". Selfies work too.

  • Pick with Mısır

    Answer 3 quick questions and let Mısır do the rest.

  • Pick your snack

    Choose what you're snacking on, and Mısır puts on a chef's hat to find the mood that goes with it.

👯Watch together

  • Watch together

    2 to 4 people pick in secret, one at a time. Nobody gets a title that fits them below 2/5, and if there's no common ground: "You tonight, them tomorrow".

  • Match game

    Everyone secretly swipes the same 10 cards. The one you all like: 💘 It's a match!

  • Play at the same time

    Two people, one phone: the screen splits in two and the top half flips around.

✨For you

  • Your 5 favorite films

    Pick 5 favorites and the "Based on your 5 films" shelf explains every suggestion.

  • Shelves

    Easy watch, Miniseries, Visual feast, plus special shelves for New Year's, Halloween and Valentine's Day.

  • Pick of the day & mood forecast

    A new pick from Mısır every day, and tonight's mood forecast.

  • Star sign shelf

    Choose your sign and get titles in its mood. Just for fun ✨

  • Mısır gets to know you

    Your list, your "How was it?" answers and every "Don't suggest this" tune the order to your taste.

🔖My list

  • My list

    Save what you like, sort it, or tap "Pick from my list tonight".

  • How was it? & mood journal

    Rate what you watched and say how you feel now. Over time you'll see which moods do you good.

  • Share

    Send a pick as an image with Mısır to WhatsApp or your Instagram story.

🍿Mısır & fun

  • A mascot with feelings

    Mısır (Turkish for "corn") changes faces with your mood, winks, wears accessories, dresses up on special days and gets sleepy after 11 PM.

  • Music & sounds

    Music that follows your mood, Mısır's wordless voice and gentle vibrations. Each one can be turned off in Settings.

  • Badges

    Collect 14 badges and grow your Mısır points.

  • 6 languages, light & dark

    Türkçe, English, Español, Português, Français, Русский.

Everything stays on your phone

  • 🙈No account

    No sign-up. Your name, list and settings live only on your phone.

  • 📷Photos never leave your phone

    Show Mısır looks at photos right on your phone. They aren't saved or sent, and nobody is identified.

  • 📶Works offline

    The title list ships inside the app. With internet, it only downloads a newer list.
